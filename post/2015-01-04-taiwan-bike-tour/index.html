At the end of 2014 I flew from San Francisco to Taiwan for a nine-day Giant round-island bike tour, from December 27 to January 4. The tour included a bike and a support van, but I brought my own, a Specialized Roubaix SL4 carbon. On the morning of Day 1 I was too happy, hit a pothole, and then saw a crack in the frame. I switched to the tour’s Giant, and my own bike rode the rest of the way in the van. We finished at 碧潭吊橋 on January 4, 2015, and then I spent a couple of days riding around Taipei on my own.

This is a look back at that trip, built from the posts and photos I shared at the time. It follows on from My Cycling Years, 2012 to 2014.

Flying over, December 24–25
#

I flew out of SFO on December 24 and arrived on December 25, Taiwan time. At Taoyuan I posted “等辦預付卡⋯” — waiting to get a prepaid card. Later I posted “抵達！” at 大湖山莊, in Taipei. I stayed at my uncle’s home.

Leaving SFO on December 24. At Taoyuan on the 25th: “等辦預付卡⋯” And my Specialized Roubaix SL4, packed in its travel case.

Day 1, December 27: New Taipei to Hsinchu
#

The riding started on December 27, at 8:48 a.m., from the New Taipei area. I had wanted to ride my own bike. On the morning of Day 1 I was too happy, hit a pothole, and then saw a crack in the frame. “悲劇了⋯還好沒摔車” — a tragedy, though luckily I didn’t crash. There was no crash, and the bike was still under warranty, so it could be replaced: “沒有crash 還在保固 所以可以換.” I switched to the tour’s Giant: “騎giant的車.”

I posted a photo of the Giant support van with my Specialized on the roof, captioned “愛駒座車環島XD” — my beloved bike was touring the island as a passenger, in the van. The ride to Hsinchu was 48.9 miles and 1,900 ft of climbing (about 78.7 km and 579 m), with 4:10 of moving time. That evening we had dinner at 華川宴, in Hsinchu.

A friend commented, “這麼硬，走台三線啊” — it was hard enough that they guessed we were on 台三線. Another asked, “捷安特鉄馬好騎嗎？” — how did the Giant ride?

December 27: “愛駒座車環島XD,” and dinner at 華川宴 in Hsinchu.

Day 2, December 28: Hsinchu to Taichung
#

December 28 started at 7:44 a.m. At 新竹市十七公里海岸風景區 I posted the Giant on the wet promenade, with the caption “New buddy!” There was a strong tailwind: “大順風！輕鬆20mph” — an easy 20 mph (about 32 km/h) — and “大概就維持在38Km/h,” holding around 38 km/h (about 24 mph).

We rode from Hsinchu through 通霄 and on to Taichung, where the van stopped at 寶天宮. The recorded ride was 69.1 miles and 928 ft of climbing (about 111.2 km and 283 m), with 4:59 of moving time. I later wrote, “有ㄧ段忘了開GPS少了十幾英里” — I had forgotten to turn on the GPS for a stretch and was short a dozen miles or so. That evening, for the Rapha 500, a riding friend shared his track from that section, and I fixed the record.

December 28, at 新竹市十七公里海岸風景區: “New buddy!” And the support van at 寶天宮 in Taichung.

Day 3, December 29: 鹿港, 西螺, and Chiayi
#

On December 29 we started at 8:36 a.m. We stopped in 鹿港, at 天后宮 and 龍山寺. There was also a stop at 溪湖糖廠. At 西螺 we were by the red span of 西螺大橋, and on 延平老街 for 碗粿. Then on to Chiayi. The ride was 48.3 miles and 384 ft of climbing (about 77.7 km and 117 m), with 3:53 of moving time. In the evening I had 東門火雞肉飯, and posted “肚子微凸 慶祝ㄧ下XD” — my stomach was a little full; time for a small celebration.

December 29: 鹿港天后宮 and 龍山寺, 西螺大橋, and 碗粿 on 延平老街.

Day 4, December 30: Chiayi to Kaohsiung
#

December 30 started with “早餐繼續吃⋯” — breakfast, and I was still eating. We passed the 北迴歸線 (Tropic of Cancer) marker in Chiayi, then 後壁 and 總爺糖廠. In Tainan I had 周氏蝦捲. We finished in Kaohsiung, by 愛河.

December 30: “早餐繼續吃⋯”; the 北迴歸線 in Chiayi; 總爺糖廠; 周氏蝦捲 in Tainan; and 愛河 in Kaohsiung.

Day 5, December 31: south to 四重溪
#

On December 31 I posted “昨晚量了下體重 還是收斂點XD” — I had weighed myself the night before, and it was time to ease up a little. We went through 潮州, for 冷熱冰, past 海角七號的小七, and 枋山, and had dinner at the 四重溪 hot springs.

December 31: 潮州冷熱冰; a map of the 恆春半島; the coast at 枋山; and dinner at 四重溪.

I also posted a Rapha Festive 500 leaderboard, with the caption “只差0.2km..LOL” — I had ridden 499.8 km from December 24 to 31, 0.2 km short of 500.

Day 6, January 1: over 壽卡 to 知本
#

New Year’s Day took us by 牡丹水庫, over 壽卡, through “大塞車⋯” at 金崙 — a big traffic jam — then 太麻里, and on to 知本.

January 1: the support van at 牡丹水庫, and a later stop along the east coast.

Day 7, January 2: 池上 and 玉里
#

January 2 started with “早安！” We went to 脫線牧場, then 伯朗大道 and the 金城武樹, and had the 池上鐵路便當. We continued to 玉里, and passed the 北迴歸線 at 春日.

January 2: “早安！”; fields near 池上; and the 池上鐵路便當.

Day 8, January 3: 瑞穗, then the train to 羅東
#

On January 3 we were in 瑞穗, and stopped at 大農大富平地森林園區. From Hualien Station we took the train to 羅東, skipping the 蘇花公路. That night I was at 羅東夜市.

January 3: 大農大富平地森林園區, and 羅東夜市.

Day 9, January 4: 北宜公路 back to Taipei
#

“要回台北囉⋯” — time to ride back to Taipei. The day’s notes warned that the road was narrow, with a lot of cars, and to watch out for scooters. We climbed to the 北宜公路制高點, with 龜山島 out in the haze, then through 坪林老街 and 石碇小格頭, and finished at 碧潭吊橋.

I posted “Done:)” with a map of the route, “CYCLING ISLAND OF TAIWAN.” In the comments I wrote, “愛駒…也算環台成功…4000 miles 光榮退休XD” — my beloved bike had made it around Taiwan too, in the van, and was retiring at 4,000 miles (about 6,400 km).

The Specialized, and a few days around Taipei
#

On January 5 I posted about the cracked frame: “這碳纖車只騎6000公里 用力煞個車 就裂開了 還是換台金屬車+減肥吧XD” — the carbon bike had only been ridden 6,000 km (about 3,730 miles); I braked hard and it cracked; maybe I should switch to a metal bike and lose some weight. I also wrote, “本來以為古典賽的用車 應該不容易壞的” — I had thought a bike meant for gran fondos wouldn’t break so easily — and “我有ㄧ台scott的鋁車了”: I already had a Scott aluminum bike.

That same day I rode around Taipei on my own. I posted “上下有點陡(劍南路》至善路》聖人橋》風櫃嘴》大湖路）還有野狗 有點怕怕” — the climbs were a bit steep, from 劍南路 to 至善路, 聖人橋, 風櫃嘴, and 大湖路, and there were stray dogs, which made me a little uneasy. The ride was 18.1 miles and 2,684 ft of climbing (about 29.1 km and 818 m), with 2:39 of moving time. In the comments I wrote, “我本以為就和Calaverasㄧ樣的陡度 也不知山上有好多野狗(下山被一隻狗追) 知道的話 沒有狗爸狗媽我是不會去的XD” — I had thought the grade would be like Calaveras, and I didn’t know there were so many stray dogs in the hills; one chased me on the descent; if I had known, I wouldn’t have gone without a dog person along. A friend commented, “環島九天還不夠。第十天還騎這麼硬。” — nine days around the island weren’t enough, and on the tenth I was still riding something this hard. I also posted “New buddy for this week:)” with a Giant I was riding that week, and stopped at 楓林橋.

January 5: “New buddy for this week:),” and a stop at 楓林橋.

On January 6 I posted “環台北…減肥XD” — a loop around Taipei, and a little weight loss. It was 51.1 miles on the river paths, with 1,345 ft of climbing (about 82.2 km and 410 m) and 4:49 of moving time. A friend asked if I had ridden alone, and I said “對” — yes. The loop took me to 福德坑, the Taipei Zoo, 艋舺, and along the 社子島 riverside path.

January 6: 福德坑, the Taipei Zoo, 艋舺, and the 社子島 riverside.
