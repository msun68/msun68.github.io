I started 2012 as a road-bike noob on a test ride. By the end of 2014 I had a few century rides behind me, two Levi’s GranFondos out of Santa Rosa, a windy coast ride in Maui, eight days of a Rapha Festive 500, and a relaxed finish at Foxy’s Fall Century.

This is a look back at those three years, built from the posts and photos I shared at the time. I already wrote up my first century, the Mount Hamilton Loop, in April 2013.

2012: a test ride, then longer days
#

On April 28 I posted a photo from a test ride: “road bike noob…test drive:)” A week later, on May 7, I bought my first bike helmet.

The rides got longer as the year went on. On June 2 I was still out with “another 18 miles to go back:)” On September 23 I did a “Sunday easy ride” of 41 miles and 1,798 ft (about 66 km and 548 m), and joked that it was the price of eating 雞排: “這是吃了雞排的代價…LOL”

Levi’s GranFondo 2012: the Medio route
#

On September 29 I rode Levi’s GranFondo in Santa Rosa, on the Medio route: 65 miles and 3,500 ft (about 105 km and 1,067 m). When I signed up I wrote, “Registered Levi’s GranFondo - Medio 65 miles 3500 feet:)”

A public Medio route on Ride with GPS is still up; that page is labeled as the 2011 course, so the line may not match the 2012 ride exactly.

The day started early: “It is 5am:)” Along the way I passed the ocean on Highway 1 — “Pacific Ocean @ Hw1” — and stopped to eat: “Another rest stop…I am full:)” Then the road tilted up. “Now…I know why there is a rest stop…the 10% is ahead(◎_◎;)” I also posted “The wall:)” and, later, “climbing is not so fun:)” At some point there were “Another 40 miles to go:)”

The Scott I rode that day, with number 7225. “Pacific Ocean @ Hw1.” A rest stop in the fog — “Another rest stop…I am full:)” “The wall:)” And afterward, in the GranFondo jersey.

Late 2012, and a trip to Maui
#

In mid-October I was looking for a buddy to ride to Lick Observatory on Mount Hamilton, a 39.9-mile route with 5,944 ft of climbing (about 64 km and 1,812 m). On November 2 I was “going to do a 75 miles ride this time:)” (about 121 km). On December 9 I did a “Sunday Mt. Diablo Ride.(strong wind and cold)” with 3,927 ft of elevation gain (about 1,197 m).

Maui, December 2012
#

On November 18 I had been thinking about cycling up Haleakalā while on vacation in Maui — 36 miles uphill, from sea level to 10,000 ft (about 58 km and 3,048 m). On December 13 I shared the route I had in mind, “Tour de Maui Stage2”: Ocean to Haleakalā Summit from Paia, 35.7 miles and 10,208 ft of climbing (about 57.5 km and 3,111 m). It looked too hard at the time, so I didn’t ride up. Instead I rode a stretch of the coast, in a strong wind, and my mom picked me up.

On December 16 I posted the bike I rode in Maui: “My ride in Maui…This is $3000 carbon bike:)” It was a rental — “租的 $40 per day” — and, as I wrote in the comments, “It is handmade in Taiwan.”

A few days later I was on the coast. The wind was fierce: “好強的風啊〜” and “風太強…” I couldn’t ride back, and my mom came to get me: “風太強騎不回去..老媽道路救援：）” I also wrote, “這個季節不太適合騎那段…落山風有點恐佈…左右夾擊…” — this wasn’t a good season for that stretch; the 落山風 was a bit terrifying, coming at me from both sides.

The rental bike at a rocky beach, then two stops along the coast: “好強的風啊〜” and “風太強…”

December 20 was my last day: “Going to enjoy my last day in Maui…biking to the north:)” The weather was better — “Alright…I am still in Maui. Nice weather:)” — and I posted “Nice MTB path…LOL” and “Back to sunny beach:)” One of the stops was Lahaina.

“Nice MTB path…LOL” “Back to sunny beach:)” And the bike beside a Lahaina sign.

On December 23 we drove up to Haleakalā. The photos I posted, all captioned “Haleakala Crater,” are from that drive.

December 23, 2012, from the drive up: “Haleakala Crater.” The summit road, and the view above the clouds.

I did ride up Haleakalā later, in 2018. That’s a story for another post.

2013: centuries, climbing, and a new bike
#

2013 picked up quickly. On February 7 I posted “Century ride!” On February 9 I climbed Sierra Road: “Complete Climbing Sierra Rd today…Satisifed. 完成6公里平均10%的陡坡”

A week later, on February 16, I had a new climbing record: 5,594 ft over 47 miles. I also wrote it as 1,700 m and 75 km: “My new record! total 5,594ft elevation 47miles / 新記錄…爬了1700公尺 75公里”

In April I wrote up my first century, the Mount Hamilton Loop. On April 7 I hit 2,000 miles and figured it was time for a new chain: “2000 miles…該換鏈了”

On May 5 I said goodbye to the bike I had been riding: “Thanks to my bike! You have served me well! I am going to upgrade you…”

By June 9, since the start of 2013, I had ridden 1,474 miles (2,372 km) and climbed 94,836 ft (28,906 m).

On August 17 I was looking at “last 10 miles..another 2000ft to climb,” and then: “Thanks to the cloud / I complete the 9 hours ride with total 93 miles/9,100 ft / 今天總共 騎了150公里 爬了2,700公尺” On August 31 I posted “My 3rd century ride this year.”

Levi’s GranFondo 2013: the Gran route
#

On October 5 I went back to Levi’s GranFondo, this time on the Gran route. The rest stops I shared were:

  • 20 mi: Monte Rio
  • 30 mi: Cazadero
  • 45 mi: King Ridge
  • 55 mi: Ritchey Ranch
  • 75 mi: Portuguese Beach
  • 80 mi: Coleman Valley (water only)
  • 85 mi: Occidental

A first flat, and the rest of 2013
#

On October 19, riding with friends, I had “My first flat tire after 2800 miles.”

On December 1 I added up the year so far: “so far in 2013, I rode total 3,130miles and up 212,802ft 今年目前總共騎了5,037公里 爬了64,862公尺— feeling accomplished.”

Sometime in mid-December I did a Garmin VIRB test ride on Calaveras. Another day that month I wrote, “late today…only did 50.53 mi…下星期一定要完成120公里的目標:)” (50.53 miles is about 81 km.)

Rapha Festive 500, December 2013
#

From December 24 onward I joined the Rapha Festive 500: “8 days of fun #festive500.” I finished with 314 miles (about 505 km).

Day 1 was 100 km, past Pulgas Water Temple and San Andreas Lake. Day 2 was Christmas: “Merry Christmas! 公休一日.” Day 3 was another 100 km, past Sunol Water Temple, the Alameda Creek Trail, and Old Mission San Jose. Day 4 followed the Coyote Creek Bike Trail to Anderson Dam; I “tried to lose some of holiday weight.” Day 5 was Tomales Bay.

Day 1, 100 km: the reflecting pool at Pulgas Water Temple, a stop at San Andreas Lake, and the temple itself.

Day 3 at Old Mission San Jose; Day 4 at Anderson Dam; and Day 5, Tomales Bay.

2014: 5,000 miles, and a relaxed last century
#

On January 10 I shared a blog post titled “Century a Week.” On March 9 I hit a new milestone: “My first 5,000 miles — in Mount Diablo.” (5,000 miles is about 8,047 km.)

On May 10 I did my first century of the year: “My first century this year. Total: 103 miles(166公里)/5,049ft(1,538公尺). The climbs are ok, but the strong wind almost killed me:)” A few days later, on May 15, I ate too much at a BBQ and joked that I needed another century that weekend.

On July 15 I signed up for the Santa Cruz Mountains Challenge 100M, with a note to myself: “Need to lose 2-3lbs this week!”

On October 18 I rode my last century of the year: “My last century this year…It is very enjoyable, relaxed ride!” That was the 2014 Foxy’s Fall Century, near Saint Helena and Putah Creek.

Foxy’s Fall Century, October 18, 2014: on the road, crossing a graffiti-covered bridge, and riding with others. “It is very enjoyable, relaxed ride!”

On December 6 I was back on the bike after a break: “幾個禮拜沒騎車 好喘！” And on December 28, at 新竹市十七公里海岸風景區 in Hsinchu, Taiwan, I posted a road bike on the coast with the caption “New buddy!” That was the second day of a nine-day Giant tour around the island, which I wrote up in Cycling Around Taiwan, 2014–2015.
